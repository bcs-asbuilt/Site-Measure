SMACNA standards checked

With "Check to SMACNA" on, each fitting is checked against these rules from the SMACNA HVAC Duct Construction Standards, Metal and Flexible (3rd edition figure numbers). A fitting that fails shows ✗ NOT TO SMACNA on the sheet, a red ✗ in the list, and the reason in the warnings. Check limits against your own copy and the job spec, which may be stricter.

3D only

Bellmouth

Standard bellmouth: the duct cut off at a 45° flat face (rectangular or round) with 12 mm bird mesh. Length is overall, from the duct connection to the furthest point of the angled face.

Under-beam transition

Round in, flattened to pass under the beam, round out. Each end has a standard collar, so overall length is two collars, two tapers and the flat.
